# Space Dominators — Sprite audit

Bosses 1–5 share `apps/space-dominators/assets/boss-fx/enemy-flight-effects.png` (four frames per row). Its rows contain the regular enemy missile, regular enemy bolt, circular wave, escort rail, mine gate and tractor cable. Boss 3 uses its existing missile artwork. Boss 4 retains its mine and explosion sprites. The asteroid belt uses its existing rocks and orbiting debris. Dynamic warnings, safe gaps and targeting geometry follow the actual attack area.
